AI assistant that drafts your Gmail replies

om ai reads your latest Gmail inbox and writes a draft reply for each conversation in the sender's language and tone, bringing it closer to your own writing style. You edit the draft, regenerate it or ignore the sender, and nothing is sent until you press Send.

Open the email assistant

Needs an om ai account and a connected Gmail. No reply is sent without your approval, and drafting may be busy at times.

What you can do

  • Drafts each reply in the sender's language and tone, using earlier messages as context
  • Uses your recent sent emails to bring drafts closer to your own style
  • Labels emails urgent, normal or low priority and moves urgent ones to the top
  • Translates emails in another language into your app language and explains a foreign draft
  • Spots a meeting mentioned in an email and adds it to your calendar in one tap
  • Voice summary of listed emails: count, urgent ones, first five senders and subjects

How it works in three steps

  1. Sign in to om ai, open the email assistant and connect your Gmail account.
  2. Review the cards: each email shows its priority, a short preview and a suggested reply.
  3. Edit or regenerate the draft and press Send, or ignore that sender so their emails stop appearing.

Frequently asked questions

Does om ai send emails without my permission?

No. Every draft stays in an editable box, and a reply goes out only when you press Send on that email. It is sent from your Gmail account inside the same conversation.

What do I need to start?

An om ai account, then connecting Gmail and allowing it to read your mail and send the replies you approve; both are required. Adding calendar events is optional, and if that permission is missing the tool asks you to connect again.

Which emails does it show?

Your inbox from the last seven days without promotions or social mail, up to ten at a time with a Load more button. If that is empty it widens to thirty days, then to your latest emails, and tells you which range it shows.

Can it make up details in my reply?

It is instructed to use only facts from the email and its thread. Where you need to decide something, such as a date or a price, it leaves a short placeholder in square brackets for you to fill. Always read the draft before sending.
