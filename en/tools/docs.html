Summarize documents and analyze contracts with AI

Upload your document as a PDF or photo, or paste its text, and the om ai Document Assistant reads and summarizes it, be it a contract, invoice, report or quote. You get an organized summary and key details such as parties, amounts and dates, then ask about any clause and get an answer based on the document itself.

Open Document Assistant

Works without an account: 3 analyses a day as a guest, 5 with a free account; follow-up questions have a daily limit.

What you can do

  • Reads PDFs and images, or document text you paste in directly
  • Identifies the type and pulls out key details like parties, amounts, dates and references
  • An organized summary of what the document is about, in your app language
  • Practical watch-outs such as obligations, deadlines, penalty clauses and key terms
  • Follow-up questions, with ready-made ones like the total amount or the start or end date
  • Bases its answers on the document alone, and tells you when the answer is not there

How it works in three steps

  1. Open the Document Assistant, then upload a PDF or a photo of the document, or paste its text.
  2. Press “Analyze document” to get the summary, the key details and the important points.
  3. Type a question about any clause or pick a ready-made one, and keep asking about the same document.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to analyze a document?

No. You can analyze up to three documents a day without signing in, and up to five with a free account. Follow-up questions have their own daily limit of up to fifteen questions.

Which files does it accept, and how big can they be?

PDFs and images up to about 3 MB, or text you paste in. Large camera photos are shrunk on your device before they are sent; if a file is still over the limit, compress or split it and upload it again.

Can it replace a lawyer or an accountant?

No. The summary and answers are AI-generated help to understand a document quickly, and the assistant can misread some details, so check the original text and consult a qualified professional before any important decision.

What language are the results in?

The summary, the key details and the answers to your questions come in the interface language you use in the app.
