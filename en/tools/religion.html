Quran Tafsir, Hadith and Dream Interpretation

The om ai Religious Insights tool puts three helpers in one place: explain a Quran verse, find and explain a hadith, and interpret a dream. Type or dictate your question, and the answer arrives laid out with headings, in the language you asked in.

Open Religious Insights

Sign-in required. Each answer counts toward your chat allowance: 20 requests a day on a free account.

What you can do

  • Verse tafsir by text or by surah and number: reason of revelation if any, meaning, lessons
  • Hadith search by text or topic: narrator, source, commonly cited grade and explanation
  • Dreams read six ways: Islamic, Christian, Jewish, Hindu-Buddhist, psychological, cultural
  • Answers in the language of your question, organized with headings and lists
  • Type or dictate with the mic button, and watch the answer appear as it is written

How it works in three steps

  1. Sign in to om ai and open Religious Insights from the menu.
  2. Pick a tab: Verse Tafsir, Hadith Lookup or Dream Interpretation, then type your question.
  3. Tap Interpret and wait a moment for the answer, organized under clear headings.

Frequently asked questions

Is the answer a fatwa?

No. Answers are AI-generated help for understanding and reflection, not a fatwa or religious ruling. Consult qualified scholars on matters of your faith, and check verse and hadith texts against their sources.

How do I find a hadith when I do not remember its wording?

Type its topic, like honoring parents, or a part you remember, and get the closest known hadith with its narrator, source, commonly cited grade and explanation. It answers from its own knowledge, not a hadith database, so check text and grade before quoting.

Does the dream interpretation follow Ibn Sirin?

The Islamic reading in each answer draws on the approach of Ibn Sirin and al-Nabulsi to dream symbols, followed by other religious readings, a psychological one and a general cultural one. Treat it as reflection, not a basis for decisions.

Is the tool free?

Yes, within a daily limit. You need an om ai account, and each answer counts toward the allowance you share with chat: 20 requests a day on a free account. When it runs out, the plans page opens, or you can wait for the next day.
