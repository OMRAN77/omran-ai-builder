Make an ad with AI from your photo and words

Describe what you want to advertise in your own words, such as a car for sale, a flat for rent, a restaurant or a salon, and the ad assistant asks for the headline, details, price and contact number. Then add a photo or skip it to get ten ready designs to adjust and download, or generate the whole ad as one AI image.

Open the Ad Studio

Sign-in is needed for the assistant and AI images. Free daily limit: up to 8 ad images and 30 assistant messages.

What you can do

  • An ad assistant gives a tip for your ad type, then asks just two questions at a time
  • Ten designs from your details: cinematic, luxe, magazine, neon, spec card and more
  • Three boards for property, villas and car showrooms: type on them, add a logo and phones
  • Drag any text, make it bigger or smaller, change colours, then download the design
  • Generate the whole ad as one AI image in square, portrait or landscape
  • Copy the ad text, ready to paste into chats and classifieds sites

How it works in three steps

  1. Pick your ad type or describe it in your own words, then answer: headline, details, price and contact.
  2. Upload one clear photo of what you are advertising, or continue without a photo if you do not have one.
  3. Choose one of the ten designs and arrange its text, then download it or generate the ad as a full AI image.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign in?

Yes, to chat with the assistant and to generate the ad as an image; without sign-in you continue with guided steps and still get the designs. A free account gets up to 8 ad images and 30 assistant messages a day, and a failed image attempt does not count.

What kinds of ads can I make?

Cars, property for sale or rent, restaurants, shops and fashion, clinics, beauty salons, services and trades, or anything else you describe. Every ad also gets three composite boards that show features, a payment plan and nearby landmarks if you give them.

Will prices and phone numbers appear correctly in the ad?

In the ten designs and the boards, text sits over the design, not inside the picture, so letters and digits stay intact, and on the boards you can tap any text to fix it. In the AI image the text is drawn in, so check the price and number before you publish.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. The page and the assistant work in 14 languages, including Arabic, English, Urdu and Hindi, and the assistant writes your ad text in the app language you chose, which the generated image uses too.
