Make AI videos from text or a photo

Describe a scene or upload a photo, and om ai turns it into a short video in landscape or portrait. Pick a ready-made trend in one tap, add text and sound to your own clips, or clean up a noisy video on your device for free.

Open the video maker

AI video needs sign-in and an active subscription and uses your plan's videos or points; on-device quality enhancement is free.

What you can do

  • Text to video: describe a scene and get a 5, 8 or 10 second clip
  • Bring photos to life: upload your picture as the star of the clip
  • One-tap trends: an old photo comes alive, a product ad, Eid and Ramadan greetings and more
  • A writing assistant drafts a story, dialogue or ad that fits your video length
  • Timed text on your video, automatic captions and a voice that reads your words
  • Free on-device enhancement: less noise, sharper edges, richer colors

How it works in three steps

  1. Open the om ai video maker and pick a section: Trends, Edit video, Cinematic, Fast video or Enhance quality.
  2. Describe the scene and pick the length and shape, or choose a trend that sets them; add your photo if needed.
  3. Tap create, confirm the cost when it appears, then watch and download your video.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI video maker free?

AI video is for subscribers and comes out of your plan's videos or your points; no points are taken before you confirm the cost. Each account can make one video every three minutes. On-device quality enhancement is free.

Can I turn my own photo into a video?

Yes. Add your photo as the star of a fast or cinematic clip, or pick a trend that asks for a photo; many trends take up to three people in one video. A portrait photo switches the clip to 9:16 automatically, and you can change it.

How long can the videos be?

Fast and cinematic clips are 5, 8 or 10 seconds, in landscape 16:9 or portrait 9:16; each trend sets its own length. Editing works on the first three minutes of your video, and the enhancer accepts videos up to 60 seconds.

Can it fix a very blurry or shaky video?

The free enhancer reduces noise, sharpens edges and improves colors, but it cannot fix heavy blur or camera shake. It runs entirely on your device, so your video is not uploaded to any server.
