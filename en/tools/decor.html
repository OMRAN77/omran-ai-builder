Redesign your room with AI interior design

Redesign your room with AI from a photo: upload it, pick one of 48 styles, and get a new photorealistic design, with the tool instructed to keep the walls, windows and camera angle. No photo? Choose a type of space or describe it in your own words and get up to four new designs.

Open the decor tool

Sign-in required. Each account gets 3 free design attempts per day.

What you can do

  • Redesign your room from a photo, aiming to keep the walls, windows and camera angle
  • 48 styles, including Najdi, modern Emirati, Andalusian, Scandinavian and luxury
  • Up to four no-photo designs for a majlis, kitchen, restaurant, cafe, pool or rooftop
  • Optional lighting, furniture, flooring, curtain, wall color and fabric choices
  • See your room in two or three styles side by side, plus a before-and-after slider
  • A gallery of idea photos from the web and written suggestions based on your room photo

How it works in three steps

  1. Sign in, open the decor tool, then upload a photo of your room or pick a type of space.
  2. Choose a decor style and, if you like, set lighting, furniture and flooring or add details in your own words.
  3. Press “Design the room”, wait a moment, then view the result and download the image you like.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a photo of my room?

No. You can upload a photo to redesign it, or pick a type of space or describe it in your own words and get up to four new designs from different angles without any photo.

Will the design change the shape of my room?

The tool is instructed to keep the room layout, walls, windows and camera angle, and change only the furniture, colors, decor and finishes. The result is a suggested visual and some details may differ from reality, so check it before you build.

How many designs can I make per day?

Sign-in is required, and each account gets 3 free design attempts per day; a failed attempt is not counted. A no-photo request counts as one attempt even if it returns four designs, while in a style comparison each style counts as one.

Does it work for majlis rooms and outdoor spaces?

Yes. It includes styles such as Arabic majlis, Najdi and modern Emirati, and the no-photo designs cover outdoor spaces like a house facade, pool, pergola, barbecue corner, rooftop and outdoor pathway.
