Try hairstyles and makeup on your photo

Change your hair color, or try a new haircut, makeup or glasses on your own photo before you decide. Upload one picture, pick a look from photo cards, then compare the result with the original and download it.

Open the style tool

Sign-in required. Free accounts get up to 3 images a day, and each edit counts as one image.

What you can do

  • Hair color, cuts and styling, including men's haircuts, braids and updos
  • Makeup, nails and henna in many designs, with henna and nail patterns varying each time
  • Beard, glasses, accessories, hijab styles, Gulf attire and heritage outfits
  • Formal ID photo, new background, wedding look, age change and an anime-style drawing
  • Merge two photos, and see hair, a beard or a heritage outfit from front, side and back
  • Before-and-after slider, two or three looks side by side, and saving favorites

How it works in three steps

  1. Upload a clear photo of yourself from the camera or gallery, or drag it in or paste it.
  2. Choose the type of edit, then a look from the photo cards. You can combine up to five picks.
  3. Press Generate, compare before and after, then download the result or save it to favorites.

Frequently asked questions

Will my face change in the result?

The tool asks for your chosen change only, then checks the result. If the person looks different it tries once more, and if that happens again it shows a message instead of the image. A clear front-facing photo gives a more accurate result.

How many images can I create per day?

A free account gets up to 3 images a day after signing in, and the allowance renews daily; an active subscription raises the limit according to the plan. When you combine several picks, each edit counts toward your allowance.

Can I apply more than one change to the same photo?

Yes. Add up to five picks: different features such as hair, makeup and glasses are combined in one image, while several looks from the same feature give one image per look, up to three images.

Can it suggest a look that suits me?

Yes. The Suggest a style button under More options reads your photo and the occasion you pick, then gives three ideas with a color tip. It does not create an image, so it does not use your daily images, and it has its own daily limit.
