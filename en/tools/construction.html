AI floor plan generator for houses and villas

Design your floor plan with AI on om ai: describe your villa, apartment block or rest house and get a 2D plan with rough room sizes, exterior and interior renders and an estimated bill of quantities. The interactive editor gives you a plan to adjust by hand and view in 3D before meeting your engineer or contractor.

Open construction tool

Sign-in required; the free plan includes up to six runs a day. Results are an early concept, not a substitute for a licensed engineer.

What you can do

  • 2D plan with rough room sizes for villas, apartments, rest houses, mosques, shops and more
  • Realistic exterior and interior renders, plus front, side, rear and aerial views
  • Interactive editor to drag and resize rooms, with a 3D tour and a split-floors view
  • Estimated bill of quantities for concrete, rebar, blocks and finishes, as a spreadsheet
  • PDF report with the plan, images, room areas, quantities and a phase timeline
  • Ten styles such as Gulf heritage, Najdi and Andalusian, with annexes like a majlis or pool

How it works in three steps

  1. Describe your project in one line, or pick the building type, floors, area and style.
  2. Choose the 2D plan, the exterior render or both, add an interior image if you like, then generate.
  3. Review it, request other angles or room interiors, then download the bill of quantities and PDF report.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the plan to apply for a building permit?

No. The result is an early concept for discussion, and the quantities are estimates. Building needs a licensed engineer and an official building permit, and every report states this clearly.

How many designs can I make per day?

You need to sign in. The free plan allows up to six runs a day: each generation counts as one, whatever outputs you pick, and each extra angle or room view counts as another. Paid plans raise this limit.

Does it tell me the construction cost?

No, it is not a pricing tool. You get estimated material quantities, room areas and a phase timeline to take to contractors when you ask for their quotes.

Can I edit the plan myself?

The generated plan image cannot be edited, but the interactive editor draws a plan from your project details where you drag and resize rooms and add rooms or floors; areas update from the dimensions, and you can render the exterior from it.
