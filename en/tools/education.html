Summarize lectures and solve homework with AI

Upload your lecture as a file or photos, or paste its text, and the om ai education tool gives you a clear summary, flashcards and a quiz at three levels. No lecture? Pick your country, grade, subject and lesson for a full explanation, or snap your homework for a step-by-step solution.

What you can do

  • Summary, flashcards and a quiz from your lecture: a PDF, photos or pasted text
  • Curriculum lesson: choose country, stage, grade, subject and lesson name
  • Ask the tutor about any point in a lesson and get a simpler explanation
  • Homework solver from a photo or text: a problem, worksheet, essay or project
  • Written questions scored out of 10, showing what you got right and what you missed
  • Spaced flashcard review, an interactive lab per lesson and a plan for your exam date

How it works in three steps

  1. Open Education in om ai and choose: upload a lecture, a curriculum lesson or the homework solver.
  2. Add the lecture file or photos with its name and subject, enter lesson details or snap homework, then start.
  3. Study the summary and flashcards, test yourself, and ask the tutor about anything you did not understand.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign in?

No. As a guest you get up to 3 lessons, 10 tutor questions and 3 homework solutions a day (building an interactive lab counts as one of the 3 lessons), saved on your device only. Signing in saves lessons to your account and raises the daily limits.

Which files can I upload as a lecture?

A PDF or DOCX file, a ZIP archive with text files, up to 10 photos of the lecture at once, or text you paste directly. Files are limited to about 10 MB; photos are resized automatically.

Does it give the full answer or teach me the method?

Both. "Full solution" shows the steps, the answer and how to check it; "Teach me step by step" reveals the steps one at a time with an optional hint. Solutions are AI-generated and can be wrong, so review them before handing in.

Does it follow my country's curriculum?

It tries to use the terms of the curriculum you choose and your grade level, but it does not see your actual textbook, so check the explanation against your book and teacher.
