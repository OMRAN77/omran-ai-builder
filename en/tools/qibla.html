Qibla direction and prayer times near you

Open the om ai Qibla tool to see today's prayer times for where you are, a countdown to the next prayer and the Qibla direction in degrees from North. On a phone with a compass, the arrow turns green when you face the Qibla.

Open the Qibla tool

No account needed for times and Qibla. Sign in to turn on prayer reminders. Accuracy depends on your device location and compass.

What you can do

  • Today's five prayer times plus sunrise, with the Hijri date
  • Live countdown to the next prayer, highlighted in the list
  • Qibla angle from North and a compass arrow that turns green when you face it
  • Calculation method set automatically by location, or pick one like Umm al-Qura or Gulf
  • Optional reminder per prayer after sign-in: at the adhan or 5, 10, 15 or 30 min before
  • Works on phone and desktop; without a compass sensor, face the angle shown

How it works in three steps

  1. Open the Qibla and prayer times tool and allow location; if you decline, an approximate location is used.
  2. Check the next prayer and countdown, and change the calculation method if times differ from your local mosque.
  3. Switch to the Qibla tab, tap Enable compass if it appears, then turn your phone until the arrow turns green.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to see prayer times and the Qibla?

No. Prayer times, the countdown and the Qibla direction open without an account. You only need to sign in to turn on prayer reminders.

How accurate is the Qibla direction?

It is calculated from your device location to the Kaaba, so accuracy depends on your location and your phone's compass. Turn on location services instead of the approximate location, and move the phone in a figure-8 to calibrate.

Why do the times differ by a few minutes from my local mosque?

Times depend on the calculation method. The tool picks one automatically by your location, and you can change it, for example to Umm al-Qura, Gulf Region or Muslim World League. For prayer, follow your mosque or official local authority.

Will the prayer reminder reach me when the app is closed?

After you sign in and tap the bell beside a prayer, a notification is sent even with the app closed if your device allows notifications, though it may sometimes arrive late. Otherwise the alert shows only while the app is open.
